# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as **Task 3 of my Data Analytics Internship at Skill Craft Technology**.

The objective of this project was to analyze employee data and develop an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a clear overview of employee attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, satisfaction levels, overtime, and work-life balance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Attrition by Department
Analyzes employee attrition across different departments, helping compare attrition levels between departments.

### 2. Age Group Distribution
Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups.

### 3. Attrition by Gender
Provides a gender-wise view of employee attrition.

### 4. Job Role & Satisfaction
Displays job roles along with satisfaction ratings to understand the distribution of attrition across satisfaction levels.

### 5. Attrition by Job Role
Highlights the top job roles based on employee attrition.

### 6. Attrition by Overtime
Analyzes attrition among employees who work overtime versus those who do not.

### 7. Work-Life Balance
Shows the distribution of employees across different work-life balance ratings.

---

## 💡 Key Observations

- The overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- **Research & Development** represents the largest share of department-level attrition in the dashboard.
- Employees in the **31–40 age group** form the largest employee group.
- The dashboard shows a higher share of male employees than female employees.
- Overtime status and work-life balance can be compared with employee attrition using the interactive visuals.

> These observations describe patterns in the dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of direct causation.
